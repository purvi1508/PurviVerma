DWG 01 / 07 — ORIGIN
Origin story

From load-bearing to learning

I started in civil engineering, learning how structures carry load and stay standing under pressure a discipline that taught me patience, precision, and respect for rigor long before I ever touched a neural network. That foundation shaped how I approach every problem since: understand the system deeply before you try to change it.

Since then, I've moved through thermodynamically consistent neural network models for complex materials, NLP research on detecting online abuse, and, most recently, building production AI safety systems in industry each one demanding the same discipline: careful experimentation, honest evaluation, and the patience to iterate until something actually works, not just looks like it does. Alongside this, I've consistently balanced deep research work with production deadlines, learning early how to manage competing priorities without letting either suffer.

What drives me is the throughline across all of it — simulation, machine learning, and LLMs, applied to problems that are both scientifically rigorous and genuinely useful in the real world. I'm not chasing novelty for its own sake; I care about research that holds up under scrutiny and work that holds up under real-world pressure. That's the same standard I bring to every deadline, every collaboration, and every problem I take on.
